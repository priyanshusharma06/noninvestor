About NonInvestor

Built for people who
find investing confusing

NonInvestor exists because most investing apps are built for experts. This one is built for everyone else.

NI
The NonInvestor Team
Independent Developer — Built with passion, for beginners
I built NonInvestor because I was once completely lost when it came to investing. Every app I tried used words I did not understand, showed numbers without explanation, and never told me what to actually do. NonInvestor is my answer to that problem. It is a free tool that talks to you like a friend, not a financial textbook. It uses live data, AI analysis, and real community opinions to help you make sense of the world of investing — without needing a degree in finance. This app will always be free. Your trust matters more than any profit.
