Frequently Asked Questions About Investing

Should I buy Apple stock right now?

NonInvestor gives you a live AI verdict on Apple stock (AAPL) updated every 60 seconds. The recommendation is based on the current price, where it sits in its 52-week range, and today's market momentum. Search AAPL above to see the current Buy, Hold or Sell signal with a plain-English explanation.

Is Bitcoin a good investment for beginners in 2026?

Bitcoin is the most established cryptocurrency but it is highly volatile — it can drop 30% in days. NonInvestor shows you the live Bitcoin price, a 5-year chart, and an AI verdict based on current market conditions. For beginners, most experts suggest starting with a small amount you can afford to lose.

What is the best investment for beginners in India?

For Indian beginners, the most commonly recommended starting points are Nifty 50 index funds via SIP, blue-chip stocks like Reliance Industries or TCS, or gold ETFs for safety. Use NonInvestor's "What Should I Buy?" tool — select "Invest in India" as your goal and it gives you personalised AI recommendations with live prices.

What is an ETF and should I invest in one?

An ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund) is a single investment that automatically spreads your money across hundreds of companies at once. For example, the S&P 500 ETF (VOO) invests in the 500 biggest US companies with one purchase. ETFs are widely considered the safest and simplest investment for beginners because they reduce risk through diversification.

How does NonInvestor's AI analysis work?

NonInvestor runs two separate AI analyses — a Buy Analyst that makes the case for buying, and a Sell Analyst that makes the case for caution. A final verdict is then generated weighing both views. The AI also uses live data signals including 52-week price position and daily momentum to ensure the verdict reflects real current conditions, not a generic default.

Is investing in stocks risky for beginners?

All investing carries risk. Individual stocks can lose significant value. ETFs reduce risk by spreading across many companies. The key rules for beginners are: only invest money you can afford to lose, start with diversified funds before individual stocks, and invest for the long term rather than trying to time the market. NonInvestor always shows risk context alongside any recommendation.

Where can I buy stocks and cryptocurrency in India?

For Indian stocks and ETFs: Zerodha, Groww, and Angel One are popular beginner-friendly platforms. For US stocks from India: INDmoney and Vested let you invest in Apple, Tesla and others in dollars. For cryptocurrency in India: CoinDCX and WazirX are the most established regulated exchanges. NonInvestor shows a "Where to Buy" section for every asset you search.

Is Reliance Industries a good stock to buy?

Reliance Industries is India's largest company, operating across oil, telecom (Jio), retail, and media. It is widely held by Indian retail investors as a core blue-chip holding. NonInvestor shows the live NSE price in Indian Rupees, a multi-year chart, and a current AI Buy/Hold/Sell recommendation. Search RELIANCE.NS to see the latest verdict.
